\section{System Overview and Architecture}

This chapter details the reasoning and explanation layer of the Drive-Test QoE Anomaly Detection \& Root Cause Analysis (RCA) system. While the upstream anomaly-detection pipeline converts raw, second-by-second LTE drive-test observations into consolidated engineer-facing incidents, the RCA layer provides the engineering intelligence that transforms those incidents into actionable, explainable, and auditable network diagnoses.

The core design philosophy rests on two strict operational principles:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{KPI as Evidence, Not Cause:} Low RSRP or high Resource Block (RB) utilization are physical measurements, not root causes. The system maps measured evidence to explicit physical mechanisms such as \textit{Weak Serving Cell} or \textit{Cell Congestion}.

\item \textbf{Deterministic Rules Over Generative Hallucination:} The root-cause decision is made by a priority-ordered, deterministic rule engine grounded in LTE engineering knowledge and 3GPP-defined radio procedures. The Large Language Model (LLM) is positioned strictly downstream as a diagnostic narrative layer; the deterministic cause is finalized before prompting, and the model is not authorized to alter the assigned cause.
\end{enumerate}

The LTE procedures and KPI semantics used by the rule base are consistent with the E-UTRA RRC, physical-layer, and MAC specifications \cite{3gpp36331,3gpp36213,3gpp36321}. The numerical diagnosis gates used in this project are engineering rules calibrated for the project workflow; they should not be interpreted as universal 3GPP optimization thresholds.

\section{RCA Reasoning Framework and Taxonomy}

To prevent a single degraded KPI, such as high BLER, from falsely triggering a diagnosis, the framework enforces a \textbf{priority-ordered evaluation hierarchy}. Causes higher in the hierarchy are evaluated first; when their defining gate conditions are satisfied, the engine can stop at the highest-priority confirmed mechanism, preserving deterministic and traceable reasoning.

Figure~\ref{rca-cause-taxonomy} summarizes the engineering cause tree around the central symptom of throughput degradation. It separates resource limitation, coverage and radio quality, cell health, mobility and access, MIMO performance, and carrier aggregation into explicit cause families supported by measurable KPI evidence.

\begin{figure}[htbp]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=0.99\linewidth]{RCA_03_throughput_cause_taxonomy.png}
\caption{Engineering RCA cause taxonomy for LTE throughput degradation and the supporting KPI/evidence branches.}
\label[fig]{rca-cause-taxonomy}
\end{figure}

\subsection{Priority 1: Resource Limitation}

\textbf{Cell Congestion (C01):} Identifies \emph{confirmed} capacity-limited cases under active download demand. The gate requires cell PRB utilization at or above 80\%, a material throughput shortfall, and healthy CQI/MCS so that a radio-quality limitation does not masquerade as congestion. Supporting evidence can additionally record high load, low rank, unavailable CA, or other capacity context.

\textbf{Poor Spectral Efficiency (C02):} Identifies scheduler under-grant when demand is present but the available radio link is not being translated into sufficient resource allocation. Low MCS, low SINR, and high BLER are retained as supporting evidence when applicable.

\subsection{Priority 2: Coverage and Radio Quality}

\textbf{Weak Serving Cell (C03):} Identifies poor serving-cell coverage through the configured weak-RSRP gate. Low RSRQ, low MCS, high BLER, and cell-edge context are retained as supporting evidence rather than treated as independent root causes.

\textbf{Interference (C04):} Identifies degraded radio quality despite adequate serving-signal power. Degraded SINR is the principal interference signature, while CQI, RSRQ, and BLER provide corroborating evidence when available.

\subsection{Priority 3: Outage / eNodeB Fault}

\textbf{Serving Cell / eNodeB Fault (C05):} Reserved for a declared Radio Link Failure while the UE's own radio conditions remain normal, indicating a likely serving-cell or hardware-side fault rather than coverage or interference. Sub-causes that require OSS, RRC-release, power, or neighbor-relation data remain non-computable when those sources are unavailable.

\textbf{Outage (C06):} Identifies a throughput or session collapse while the UE's own radio conditions remain normal. This branch deliberately represents a general network-side service disruption: drive-test evidence alone does not determine whether the serving site, a neighboring site, transport, or another network element is at fault. Supporting evidence such as a serving-cell utilization increase or a neighbor-availability drop is retained as an investigation lead rather than treated as independent confirmation.

\subsection{Priority 4: Mobility}

\textbf{Handover Problem (C08):} Captures handover-adjacent degradation through supporting patterns such as ping-pong handover, recurring wrong-neighbor behavior, late handover, and high-speed mobility context.

\textbf{Access Problem (C09):} Captures Random Access Channel (RACH) failure or contention signatures. More specific explanations that require uplink interference or RACH transmit-power telemetry remain non-computable when those measurements are unavailable.

\subsection{Priority 5: MIMO}

\textbf{MIMO Rank Fallback (C10):} Identifies poor spatial multiplexing when link quality is strong but the Rank Indicator falls back to Rank 1, pointing toward antenna-path, correlation, MIMO-capability, or adaptation issues.

\subsection{Priority 6: Carrier Aggregation}

\textbf{Weak Secondary Cell (C11):} Identifies active Carrier Aggregation whose secondary carrier is limited by poor SCell radio quality. Low SCell SINR, low SCell RSRP, and low or zero SCell RB allocation are retained as supporting evidence.

\textbf{SCell Release / Deactivation (C12):} Retained in the taxonomy for secondary-carrier deactivation during an active session, but currently marked non-computable because the incident aggregate does not contain the within-episode SCell activation/deactivation timeline required to distinguish a true mid-session release.

\textbf{CA Inactive / Disabled (C13):} Identifies sessions in which Carrier Aggregation never becomes active and the UE remains on a single carrier, leaving no aggregated bandwidth or SCell radio evidence available.

\subsection{Priority 7: Conservative Fallback Tier}

\textbf{Elevated Load (C01\_f):} A lower-confidence resource-pressure classification that remains taxonomically under Cell Congestion but is evaluated only after Priorities 1--6. It requires confirmed demand, a substantial throughput shortfall, healthy CQI/MCS, cell PRB utilization of at least 50\%, and median UE RB usage of at least 60 RBs. Because confirmed C01 is evaluated first at the 80\% cell-load gate, this tier effectively captures elevated-but-not-confirmed saturation and cannot override a more specific MIMO or Carrier Aggregation diagnosis.

\textbf{Radio Limited (C14):} A lower-priority fallback for degraded link adaptation, such as elevated BLER or low CQI, when no higher-priority cause has already been confirmed.

\textbf{Inconclusive (C15):} The definitive last-resort classification when active demand is not confirmed or no condition in the preceding hierarchy matches. It is retained for auditability but is not interpreted as a confirmed network root cause.

\section{Deterministic RCA Engine Execution Flow}

The classification script (\texttt{classify\_incidents.py}) executes a rule-driven pipeline at runtime:

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Load Artifacts:} Parses \texttt{causes.json}, \texttt{kpi\_rules.json}, \texttt{solutions.json}, and the incident-level RCA handoff.
\item \textbf{Extract Telemetry:} Builds a flat feature dictionary from raw and derived incident KPIs using the classifier's \texttt{extract()} stage.
\item \textbf{Apply the Universal Demand Precondition:} Incidents without a valid expected-throughput opportunity are routed directly to the conservative Inconclusive fallback rather than being forced into a network-fault class.
\item \textbf{Evaluate Priorities 1--4:} Resource Limitation, Coverage/Radio Quality, Outage/eNodeB Fault, and Mobility are evaluated first in rule order.
\item \textbf{Evaluate Priorities 5--6:} If no earlier rule fires, the engine evaluates the more specific MIMO and Carrier Aggregation signatures.
\item \textbf{Evaluate the Priority-7 Fallback Tier:} If Priorities 1--6 find no match, the engine checks Elevated Load (C01\_f), then Radio Limited (C14), and finally Inconclusive (C15).
\item \textbf{Collect Supporting Evidence:} Once a parent rule or mobility alternative fires, supporting-evidence conditions are evaluated independently and attached to the selected diagnosis.
\item \textbf{Format Reason and Remediation:} The selected \texttt{reason\_template} is populated with measured KPI values, then matching actions and references are retrieved from \texttt{solutions.json}.
\item \textbf{Emit Structured Output:} Writes the deterministic diagnosis, supporting evidence, KPI evidence, and recommended solution to the final JSON artifact.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Conservative Congestion Attribution:} Confirmed Cell Congestion (C01) accounts for \textbf{85 incidents (6.2\%)}. \item \textbf{Coverage Remains the Most Frequent Confirmed Specific Cause:} Weak Serving Cell (C03) is assigned to \textbf{250 incidents (18.1\%)}. This indicates that serving-signal weakness remains a major physical explanation for throughput degradation in the evaluated drive-test population.

C15 is deliberately retained as an audit outcome rather than counted as a confirmed network root cause, avoiding unsupported diagnoses when demand or evidence is insufficient.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Downstream LLM Diagnostic Narration}

The Large Language Model (LLM) operates strictly as a text-generation layer downstream of the deterministic RCA engine. It translates structured JSON verdicts into concise diagnostic reports for Network Operations Center (NOC) engineers.

\subsection{Recommended Prompting Structure}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Context \& Symptom:} State the incident ID, timestamp, serving cell, and throughput drop.
\item \textbf{Deterministic Verdict:} State the primary cause name and cause ID (C01--C15, with C07 unused in the current taxonomy).
\item \textbf{KPI Evidence:} Cite the exact numerical KPI values that triggered the decision rule.
\item \textbf{Reasoning Explanation:} Explain why the KPI combination supports this physical mechanism.
\item \textbf{Action Plan:} Present the recommended remediation steps from \texttt{solutions.json}.
\end{enumerate}

\begin{warningbox}{System Guardrail}
The LLM prompt strictly instructs the model to explain the deterministic verdict. It is forbidden from altering the assigned \texttt{cause\_id}, inventing unmeasured KPIs, or proposing remedies outside \texttt{solutions.json}.
\end{warningbox}

\section{Future Work and Enhancements}

\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Row-Level Sequential RCA:} Extend the engine from incident-level aggregates to row-level time-series data to capture real-time state transitions, such as mid-session SCell drops.

\item \textbf{OSS \& Alarm Integration:} Ingest eNodeB hardware alarms, MME paging logs, and backhaul status counters to resolve C05/C06 outage sub-causes.

\item \textbf{Dynamic Cell-Specific Baselines:} Replace selected global engineering thresholds with cell-specific historical percentiles ($p_{10}/p_{50}$) once sufficient historical data is available.

\item \textbf{Enhanced Evidence Scoring:} Implement a multi-factor confidence score based on the number and independence of supporting KPI indicators.

\item \textbf{LLM Verification Guard:} Implement an automated validation check that compares generated text against the source JSON to ensure factual alignment.
\end{enumerate}

\section{RCA Chapter Conclusion}

This contribution establishes an auditable, rule-driven Root Cause Analysis framework for LTE drive-test throughput anomalies. By enforcing a deterministic decision layer backed by a modular three-JSON knowledge base, the system converts measured telemetry into clear engineering diagnoses linked to actionable remediation steps.

Applied to the 1,382-incident evaluation dataset, the final hierarchy identifies \textbf{Weak Serving Cell} as the most frequent confirmed specific network cause (250 incidents, 18.1\%), while separating \textbf{confirmed Cell Congestion} (85 incidents, 6.2\%) from the lower-confidence \textbf{Elevated Load} tier (149 incidents, 10.8\%). The engine also retains 266 \textbf{Inconclusive} classifications (19.2\%) as an explicit audit fallback rather than forcing unsupported network diagnoses. The downstream LLM layer converts these structured verdicts into clear diagnostic prose without sacrificing deterministic rigor.
