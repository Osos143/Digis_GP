\section{Meeting Outcome and Final Scope}

\begin{successbox}{Main Session Decision}
The first RCA implementation will focus on \textbf{throughput degradation} using the drive test data flow discussed in Session 2. Handover failure RCA will remain as \textbf{future work} after the first stable throughput RCA pipeline is completed.
\end{successbox}

The main target is to convert drive test measurements into a clear RCA output: detect abnormal KPI behavior, monitor the repeated anomaly patterns, reason about the most probable cause, then represent the final relation between the anomaly and the cause in a compact structure.

\begin{warningbox}{Important Data Separation}
The \textbf{drive test data} and \textbf{planning data} are completely independent. The site locations are different, so planning data must \textbf{not} be used to validate, prove, or directly correct drive test anomalies. Planning analysis will be handled as a separate workstream and will produce an independent planning output.
\end{warningbox}

\begin{infobox}{Drive Test RCA Flow from Session 2}
\centering
\textbf{Drive Test KPIs} $\rightarrow$ \textbf{Feature Preparation} $\rightarrow$ \textbf{Anomaly Detection using Multiple Models} $\rightarrow$ \textbf{Anomaly Monitoring} $\rightarrow$ \textbf{KPI Reasoning / LLM-Assisted Explanation} $\rightarrow$ \textbf{Cause-Anomaly Mapping} $\rightarrow$ \textbf{Final RCA Output}
\end{infobox}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\footnotesize
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{P{2.8cm}Y Y}
\toprule
\textbf{Scope Area} & \textbf{Role in This Phase} & \textbf{Expected Output} \\
\midrule
Drive test anomaly detection & Detect throughput degradation and abnormal behavior in supporting KPIs using statistical and ML-based methods. & Structured anomaly log with KPI name, severity, affected segment/cell if available, and evidence. \\
\midrule
Anomaly monitoring & Track repeated anomaly patterns across route segments, samples, time windows, or cells available in the drive test logs. & Monitored anomaly cases showing persistence, repetition, and priority. \\
\midrule
KPI reasoning & Explain the probable cause using KPI behavior, telecom logic, and possible LLM-assisted reasoning. & Cause category, explanation, confidence, and supporting KPI evidence. \\
\midrule
Cause-anomaly representation & Connect each drive test anomaly with its most probable cause and present it in a clean RCA structure. & Final RCA table, relation map, or dashboard-ready schema. \\
\midrule
Independent planning analysis & Analyze planning data separately because it does not represent the same sites/locations as the drive test data. & Separate planning table/report, not a validation layer for drive test RCA. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\caption{Updated scope with drive test RCA as the main flow and planning as an independent stream.}
\end{table}

\section{Detailed Drive Test Technical Flow}

The drive test flow is the main implementation path for the first RCA version. The output should start from measured KPI behavior and end with a reasoned RCA record.

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\footnotesize
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{P{2.5cm}Y Y}
\toprule
\textbf{Stage} & \textbf{Purpose} & \textbf{Output} \\
\midrule
D1: KPI preparation & Select the target KPI, mainly throughput, and prepare supporting KPIs such as signal strength, signal quality, interference/quality indicators, retransmission/error indicators, and any available cell or route identifiers. & Clean feature table for drive test analysis. \\
\midrule
D2: Multi-model anomaly detection & Apply more than one detection approach so the team is not dependent on a single model. Suitable options include thresholds, statistical deviation, clustering, isolation-based methods, or other ML methods depending on the available columns. & Anomaly log with score, severity, and evidence. \\
\midrule
D3: Anomaly monitoring & Group and monitor detected anomalies to identify repeated or persistent degradation instead of treating every abnormal sample as a separate problem. & Monitored anomaly cases with repetition and priority. \\
\midrule
D4: KPI reasoning & Interpret the anomaly using KPI patterns. For example, low throughput with weak coverage indicators may suggest coverage limitation, while low throughput with poor quality/interference indicators may suggest interference or radio quality degradation. & Probable cause category with explanation and confidence. \\
\midrule
D5: RCA representation & Connect the anomaly, supporting KPIs, reasoning result, and recommended action in one readable structure. & Final drive test RCA record. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\caption{Main drive test RCA flow to be implemented first.}
\end{table}

\begin{successbox}{Drive Test RCA Priority}
Throughput degradation is the first priority. The reasoning should focus on explaining why throughput became abnormal using only drive test KPI evidence and domain logic. Planning results are independent and should not be used as proof for drive test RCA.
\end{successbox}

\section{Expected Drive Test RCA Output Structure}

\begin{table}[H]
\centering
\scriptsize
\begin{tabularx}{\textwidth}{P{3.35cm}Y}
\toprule
\textbf{Field} & \textbf{Description} \\
\midrule
\colname{anomaly\_id} & Unique ID for the detected throughput abnormality. \\
\colname{record\_source} & Main RCA output should be drive-test-based; planning records stay separate. \\
\colname{kpi\_name} & Main affected KPI, mainly throughput in the first phase. \\
\colname{segment/time/cell} & Affected route segment, time window, or serving cell if available. \\
\colname{severity} & Anomaly strength from statistical or model-based score. \\
\colname{supporting\_kpis} & KPI values supporting the anomaly and reasoning decision. \\
\colname{probable\_cause} & Cause category such as coverage limitation, interference/poor quality, congestion-like behavior, configuration-related behavior, or unknown pending validation. \\
\colname{reasoning\_summary} & Short explanation of why this cause is likely from the KPI pattern. \\
\colname{confidence} & Confidence level from the reasoning layer. \\
\colname{recommended\_action} & Suggested validation or optimization action. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\caption{Minimum recommended structure for the drive test RCA output.}
\end{table}

Handover failure RCA will remain outside the immediate scope. It can be added later using the same drive test structure: event/anomaly detection, monitoring, reasoning, cause mapping, and final RCA representation.
\end{warningbox}
